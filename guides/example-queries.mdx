---
title: Example queries
description: Natural language queries you can ask once the Axion MCP Server is connected.
---

Once the Axion MCP Server is connected to your LLM client, you can ask financial questions in plain English. The server translates your questions into API calls and returns structured data. Below are example queries organized by category.

## Stock research

- "What is AAPL's current stock price?"
- "Show me a summary of Microsoft's stock performance over the last month."
- "What is the 52-week high and low for NVDA?"
- "Compare the year-to-date performance of GOOGL and META."
- "What is Tesla's market capitalization right now?"
- "Show me the pre-market price for Amazon."
- "What are the most actively traded stocks today?"

## Crypto

- "What is Bitcoin's price right now?"
- "Show me Ethereum's price change over the last 24 hours."
- "What is the total market cap of the top 10 cryptocurrencies?"
- "Compare BTC and ETH performance over the last 7 days."
- "What is Solana trading at currently?"
- "Show me the current fear and greed index for crypto markets."
- "What are the top gaining cryptocurrencies today?"

## ETFs

- "What is the current price of SPY?"
- "Show me the top holdings of QQQ."
- "How has VTI performed over the last year?"
- "What ETFs have the highest trading volume today?"
- "Show me the expense ratio and AUM for IVV."
- "What sector ETFs are outperforming the market this week?"
- "Compare the dividend yields of SCHD and VYM."

## Financials & earnings

- "When does Apple next report earnings?"
- "What were Amazon's revenue and earnings per share last quarter?"
- "Show me Netflix's income statement for the most recent fiscal year."
- "What is MSFT's price-to-earnings ratio?"
- "How does Nvidia's gross margin compare to AMD's?"
- "Show me the free cash flow for Alphabet over the last four quarters."
- "What companies are reporting earnings this week?"
- "What is Meta's debt-to-equity ratio?"

## News & sentiment

- "Show me the latest news for Tesla."
- "What is the market sentiment around Apple right now?"
- "Are there any recent analyst upgrades or downgrades for AMZN?"
- "What major financial news broke today?"
- "Show me recent news about the Federal Reserve."
- "What are analysts saying about Nvidia's outlook?"
- "Has there been any recent insider news about Microsoft?"

## Economics

- "What is the current US inflation rate?"
- "Show me the latest unemployment figures."
- "What did the Fed say at the last FOMC meeting?"
- "What is the current federal funds rate?"
- "How has the US dollar index moved this month?"
- "Show me the latest GDP growth figures for the US."
- "What are the current 10-year Treasury yields?"
- "When is the next CPI release date?"

## SEC filings

- "Show me Apple's most recent 10-K filing."
- "Has Tesla filed any 8-K forms recently?"
- "What did Amazon disclose in its latest proxy statement?"
- "Show me the insider transaction disclosures for MSFT this quarter."
- "Pull up Nvidia's most recent 10-Q filing."
- "What did the CEO of Meta disclose in their last Form 4 filing?"
- "Show me any recent S-1 filings in the technology sector."

## Ownership & insiders

- "Have any insiders bought or sold NVDA shares recently?"
- "What percentage of Tesla is held by institutional investors?"
- "Show me recent insider selling activity for Amazon."
- "Which hedge funds have the largest positions in Microsoft?"
- "Has Elon Musk filed any recent Form 4 disclosures?"
- "What is Berkshire Hathaway's current stake in Apple?"
- "Show me recent 13-F filings mentioning Nvidia."
